## Books

### Foundational ML & Deep Learning

**Deep Learning** — Goodfellow, Bengio & Courville  
The definitive theoretical reference for deep learning. Dense but essential.  
[Free online](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/)

---

**Hands-On Machine Learning with Scikit-Learn, Keras & TensorFlow** — Aurélien Géron  
Best practical introduction to ML. Great for beginners and intermediates.  
[O'Reilly](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/hands-on-machine-learning/9781492032632/)

---

**Designing Machine Learning Systems** — Chip Huyen  
Essential reading for production ML. Covers the full lifecycle.  
[O'Reilly](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/designing-machine-learning/9781098107956/)

---

### Mathematics for ML

**Mathematics for Machine Learning** — Deisenroth, Faisal & Ong  
Linear algebra, calculus, and probability for ML practitioners.  
[Free online](https://mml-book.github.io/)
